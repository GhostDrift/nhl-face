1.0.0: First release

Hockey-face brings your team to your wrist. Pick from all 32 teams and the face takes on their colors.

• Next game, live scores with the period and clock, and final scores
• Goal banner with the scorer, assists and time when your team scores
• Record, points, and division and league rank
• Playoff series tracking, all the way to the Cup
• Away colors on away game days, and classic color schemes for teams with retro looks
• Optional vibrations for goals, periods and the final horn

Works on the Pebble Time, Pebble Time Steel, Pebble 2 and Pebble Time 2.
